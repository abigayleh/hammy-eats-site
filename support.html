Support

Something broken, or a question about the game? Email abigaylehickey@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

Common questions

No customers are coming in. Customers only sit down when there's food on a display. Tap the oven and bake a batch.

How do I move or sell furniture? Press and hold it to lift it, then drag it, rotate it, sell it or place it.

How do I get new recipes? Serve customers to level up, then buy new recipes from the Recipes tab.

Can I move my café to another phone? Not yet. The save lives only on the device it was played on.

How do I start over? Deleting and reinstalling the app starts a new café.

How do I rename my café? Settings › Café name.
