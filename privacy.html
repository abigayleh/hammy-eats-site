Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 November 2026.

Hammy Eats does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your café — its name, acorns, level, recipes, furniture and settings — is saved only on your iPhone. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes the save.

What the game does not do

There are no accounts and no sign-in. The game contains no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party services, and it makes no network requests while you play.

Children

Because the game collects nothing, it collects nothing from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
